Today, PostHog Code enters beta. It's a desktop app that runs coding agents on top of your product data. The obvious stuff ships itself. The tricky stuff shows up as a prioritized to-do list for you to steer.We built it to chase one idea: self-driving product development.How we're defining self-drivingA self-driving product can prompt itself. It understands your codebase, your data, and your users. It proposes and ships work on its own, inside of guardrails you set.The self in self-driving isn't autonomy from the engineer. It's autonomy from user instruction as the starting point.
